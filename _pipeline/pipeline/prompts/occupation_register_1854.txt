The whole book is one OCCUPATION REGISTER: occupation headings in capitals, in alphabetical order,
each followed by the practitioners. Every practitioner line has four columns:

Addresses use the old Groningen ward numbering: a WIJK LETTER (A–Z; "IJ" is one letter) plus a
number within that ward. The number may carry a letter suffix (95a) or a superscript (26², write "26-2").
